This page explains what personal information SmartCart BD collects, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and password (stored only as a secure hash), or your Google profile if you log in with Google.
  • Orders: name, phone number, email, delivery address, the products you buy and how you pay. Card and mobile-wallet details are entered on the SSLCommerz payment page; we never see or store them.
  • Your cart: what is in it and when it last changed. If you type your email at checkout but don't finish the order, we may send you one reminder.
  • Optional: saved addresses, wishlist, reviews and photos, questions, price alerts, newsletter subscription and push notifications.
  • Technical: cookies needed for login, the cart and security (to block repeated failed logins), plus your theme and digit preferences.

How we use it

  • To deliver your orders and keep you updated by email, in-site notifications and, if you turn them on, push notifications.
  • To run reward points and referrals, and to show recommendations based on what is in the shop.
  • To protect accounts and stop fraud.
  • To send offers, only if you subscribe to the newsletter. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

Who we share it with

Only with the services needed to complete your order: the courier (name, phone and address), the payment gateway (order amount and contact details) and our email provider. We do not sell your information.

Your choices

  • Edit or delete saved addresses from your profile, and turn push notifications off on the notifications page.
  • Unsubscribe from the newsletter with the link in any newsletter email.
  • Ask us to delete your account. Order records are kept as long as the law requires for accounting.
