If something is wrong with your order, or you changed your mind, you can ask for a return within {{ return_days }} days of delivery.

How to return

  1. Open the order in My orders and choose Request a return (shown for {{ return_days }} days after delivery).
  2. Tell us the reason and add any details. We reply within 2 working days.
  3. Once approved, we arrange pickup or tell you where to send the item.

What can be returned

  • Wrong, damaged or defective items: always, and we pay the return delivery.
  • Change of mind: unused items in the original packaging with all tags and accessories. The return delivery charge is deducted from the refund.
  • Not returnable for hygiene reasons: opened personal care items, innerwear and earphones with a broken seal (unless faulty).

Refunds

  • Online payments are refunded to the same bKash, Nagad or card account within 7-10 working days after we receive the item.
  • Cash on Delivery orders are refunded to your bKash or Nagad number, or a bank account.
  • Reward points used on the order are returned to your account.

Cancelling an order

You can cancel an order yourself from the order page until it is shipped. The items go back on sale and any points you used are returned.
